# Brag Plan: MusicSM

## What is this app?
A dark, glassmorphic Android music‑streaming app — real audio from YouTube, with offline caching, true overlapping crossfade, live synced lyrics, and taste‑based recommendations. A full Spotify/Apple‑Music‑class player, built solo.

## The angle
"Everything a music app should do — actually built." Rapid-fire, clean feature reveals over the app's own coral‑on‑black aurora‑glass look. Not marketing fluff: show the real Now Playing, real shelves, real download waves. The flex is the *density* of polish shipped by one person.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen → the coral **MusicSM** wordmark snaps in over a soft aurora bloom, and the Now Playing album art breathes to life with the animated seek bar flowing coral→lavender. One line: "Your music, done right."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Now Playing** — breathing album art, flowing multi‑hue seek bar, frosted hue play button.
- **Home** — personalized shelves sliding in: "Recommended for you", "Trending now", "From artists you follow".
- **Offline** — the wavy download bar filling + "Downloaded" — cache + downloads.
- **Crossfade** — two waveforms overlapping into one (true crossfade) + "No gaps. Ever."

## Outro / punchline
Feature words rush in as chips (Streaming · Offline · Crossfade · Lyrics · Recommendations) → collapse into the wordmark: **MusicSM** + "Built from scratch."

## User flow worth showing
entry → Home recommendations shelf → tap a song → Now Playing (breathing art + flowing seek) → download for offline (wavy bar). Centerpiece = Now Playing + the offline download.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: sleek premium music‑app launch film
- Interpretation: smooth clean reveals, confident holds, feature‑card clarity; energetic but never chaotic. Motion carries the pace, text always holds long enough to read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans (400/600)
- Strongest visual element: Now Playing — square album art with a flowing coral→lavender→teal capsule seek bar and a frosted‑glass round play button; aurora coral/lavender radial blooms on a near‑black background.

## Share copy (draft)
Built a full music app from scratch — streaming, offline cache, true crossfade, live lyrics, taste‑based recs. Meet MusicSM. 🎧

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, modern music bed — it's a music app, so music leads.
- Music: bundled upbeat/electronic track, medium energy; steady groove.
- Music treatment: start at 0s, confident level, gentle fade‑out in the last ~0.6s.
- Music cue guidance: use the bundled track's cue preset if present; target strong cues for the wordmark hook, the Now Playing reveal, and the outro collapse. Beat‑grid the Home shelf reveals (every other beat, hold each label to the read floor). If no preset, detect at composition time.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the seek bar / aurora glow breathe with music energy; no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion‑matched — a soft tick on each shelf/chip arrival, a light woosh on scene wipes, one clean tap when the song is selected.
- Audio-coupled moments: song tap, shelf card arrivals, feature chips, download‑bar fill.
- Restraint rule: no loud stingers, no cluttered SFX; audio stays premium and clean.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook / wordmark — 3s
Near‑black with coral + lavender aurora radial blooms. "MusicSM" wordmark scales in (Plus Jakarta 800, coral), subtitle "Your music, done right." fades under it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: music starts on a downbeat; a soft swell under the wordmark.
Audio-coupled idea: strong cue aligned to wordmark snap.
Music: upbeat electronic bed begins.
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Now Playing — 5s
Recreate Now Playing: centered square album art (rounded), gently breathing (scale pulse); below it a capsule **seek bar** with a flowing coral→lavender→teal fill; a round frosted play button. Title/artist lines above the bar. Caption chip: "Now Playing".
Sequential/interaction: yes — a cursor/tap lands on the play button; the seek fill begins flowing.
Audio intent: groove settles; subtle glow breathes with the beat.
Audio-coupled idea: clean tap SFX on the play button.
Music: full bed.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Home shelves — 4s
Dark home feed; three shelf labels + album cards slide in one by one: "Recommended for you", "Trending now", "From artists you follow", each with 3 small rounded art cards.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 shelves arrive one by one, cards pop per shelf.
Audio intent: each shelf lands on a beat with a soft tick.
Audio-coupled idea: beat‑grid reveals, hold each label ~0.8s.
Music: full bed.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Offline + Crossfade — 4s
Split beat: (a) the **wavy download bar** fills 0→100% with a "Downloaded ✓" tick; (b) two audio waveforms slide together and overlap into one with the line "True crossfade — no gaps."
Sequential/interaction: yes — download wave fills, then crossfade waves merge.
Audio intent: the two music phrases blend (mirrors crossfade) as the waves overlap.
Audio-coupled idea: rising fill tone on the download bar.
Music: full bed, brief blended moment.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Feature chips → outro — 4s
Feature chips rush in and settle in a row: Streaming · Offline · Crossfade · Lyrics · Recommendations. They collapse/fade into the centered **MusicSM** wordmark + "Built from scratch."
Sequential/interaction: yes — chips arrive quickly, hold as a set, then collapse.
Audio intent: final swell; music fades out cleanly on the wordmark.
Audio-coupled idea: soft tick per chip; last chip lands on a strong cue.
Music: resolve + ~0.6s fade‑out.
Transition mood: soft settle → end
